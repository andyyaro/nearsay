# Code of conduct

nearsay is a small public project that lets a phone start a coding session on a Mac. Anyone who
uses it, reports a problem with it or contributes to it should be able to do that without being
harassed, belittled or ignored.

## What is expected

- Treat people with respect, including people you disagree with.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Keep claims honest. Say what you tested and on what (Mac model, macOS and iOS versions), and say
  when something is a guess.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's keys, host names, network details or personal data, and
  remove them from logs before you share them.
- Assume people are new rather than careless, and help them find what they need.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sexual language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults and repeated hostile comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Continuing to contact someone who has asked you to stop.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions in this repository, and anywhere someone is
speaking for the project.

## Reporting

Email **andyyaro25@gmail.com**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.
You can also report a comment or account to GitHub directly from the `...` menu on the comment.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or warn,
temporarily block or permanently block the person responsible. The person who reported it will
hear what was done, without details that would expose anyone else.
