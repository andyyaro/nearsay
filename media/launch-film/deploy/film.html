nearsay. A voice launcher for Claude Code.

Tap the sticker, and say what you want done. When you stop talking, the phone sends it to your Mac. Claude Code opens in a terminal, in the right project, with your words as the task. And the same session shows up in the Claude app on your phone, so the lid can stay closed.

The words go over SSH, with a key that can start one program, and nothing else. They travel as data, never through a shell. And only the Mac decides the folder, the model and the permissions.

Start with a project's name, and the task goes there. Otherwise, it goes to your default project.

Three Shortcut actions on the phone. Standard-library Python on the Mac. 111 tests.

nearsay. Hold your phone near, and say the task.

The phone's screen is a screen recording of one real tap, played in real time. The Mac was recorded with its lid open and is shown shut; its Terminal is redrawn, each line at the second it appeared, with the session link, user name and usage details left out. With the lid shut, the Mac must stay awake.
